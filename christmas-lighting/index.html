More than lights

Designed around the trees. Built around the memories.

Christmas lighting is more than wrapping a roofline. We consider the natural architecture of your trees, the character of your home or commercial property, and the experience you want to create.

Our crews prepare trees before installing lights, with appropriate pruning and tree-care assessment. Then we illuminate their structure—from trunk to branch tips—to create depth, balance and extraordinary nighttime displays.

Our background includes ISA Certified Arborists, TCIA Certified Plant Health Care Technicians, JLB Canopy Management Certification, Utah contractor licensing and B.S./M.S. horticultural education.

A Utah Christmas tradition

Since 1965. Generations of Christmas memories.

Behunin Tree's Christmas decorating heritage includes Temple Square, the Delta Center, Jordan Commons, Draper's Tree of Life, the 2002 Winter Olympics and the Utah State Capitol Christmas tree.

Our Utah tree and horticultural roots reach back to 1860. That experience shapes how we approach living trees, landscape safety, and the art of lighting a property beautifully.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Christmas lighting design consultation free?

No. Behunin Tree charges a design consultation fee. We discuss recommendations verbally and provide a customized 2D design and installation estimate following the consultation.

Do you decorate homes and commercial properties?

Yes. We design Christmas lighting for residential properties and commercial, civic and landmark settings.

Do you prepare trees before installing lights?

Yes. Tree assessment and appropriate pruning are part of our approach to protecting tree structure and creating a better display.

Can you maintain and remove the lights?

Maintenance, removal and storage can be discussed as part of the proposed scope of work and contract.
